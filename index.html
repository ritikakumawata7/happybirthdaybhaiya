For my brother.

Dear Bhaiya,

I know we don't always stop and put everything into words, but I wanted this birthday to have at least one place where I did.

Thank you for all the normal days that somehow turned into good memories — the jokes, the conversations, the random nonsense, and even the moments where we completely annoyed each other.

Having a brother means having someone who is part of your story long before you realise how important that story is.
